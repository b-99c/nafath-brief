## Overview

Interoperability of software, mobile apps, data, artificial intelligence systems, and internet connections is essential to a national service. If any of these areas are weak, they can put the service or users at risk. Secure software practices help to secure settings and secrets, and mobile security checks help to find weaknesses in the application. Artificial intelligence systems also need to be protected from attacks that could interfere with their inputs or training data. Good networking ensures that Internet traffic is routed safely to the actual service. Cybersecurity teams need to identify and fix serious vulnerabilities, and a software bill of materials helps them understand what components the service relies on. Together, these fields help the organization reduce risk, respond to problems and keep the service trustworthy and available.
